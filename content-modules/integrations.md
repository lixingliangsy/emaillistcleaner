# ListClean — Integrations

## Export targets
- **Copy-paste** — keep-list and remove-list as plain text, pasted straight into any ESP.
- **CSV-ready** — one address per line, importable to Mailchimp, ConvertKit, Brevo, Beehiiv, etc.

## API & webhooks
- **Bulk API** (Pro) — submit a list and receive a structured cleaning report (`source: 'Model-assisted'` when live).
- Webhook ingestion is on the roadmap; today lists are submitted via the tool or API.

## Honesty note on connectors
Some ESP "one-click" connectors shown in plans may not be shipped yet. Verify the current connector list on the pricing page before relying on a direct sync. We do not claim a connector exists until it is live.
